Creative Options Guide

Visual Color Filters

Apply specialized color grading formulas to give each photo or video a unique vibe:

Cinematic Cool Deep teal shadow tones with warm highlights. Creates a modern high-contrast cinematic film grade.
Energetic Vivid High saturation and boosted contrast. Perfect for action shots, travel, or bright outdoor scenes.
Vintage Warm Warm yellow highlights, desaturated greens, and lowered contrast for a nostalgic film print look.
Noir High-Contrast Dramatic black-and-white grading with deep shadows and bright highlight definitions.
Cyberpunk Neon Intense cyan shadow tinting and magenta highlights. Captures a futuristic neon city aesthetic.
Dreamy Bloom Adds a soft-focus lens glow that bleeds highlights together, creating a dreamy fantasy look.
Golden Sunset Applies warm orange and golden-hour tones.
Cool Pine Applies deep forest green tones and boosts contrast for a moody outdoor look.
Retro Polaroid Fades black levels upwards, desaturates highlights, and creates a Polaroid-style border.
Cinematic Letterbox Applies cinematic grading with black horizontal letterbox bars.
Cyberpunk Border Intense cyberpunk neon colors with a glowing cyan border.
Vintage Grain Warm tones combined with randomized film grain.
Vaporwave Dream Magenta, violet and cyan tones for a vaporwave aesthetic.

Camera Motion & VFX

Control how the camera moves across the media over its display duration:

Slow Zoom In Slowly scales into the subject.
Slow Zoom Out Slowly reveals the surrounding environment.
Pan Left-to-Right Creates a cinematic horizontal camera movement.
Pan Right-to-Left Slides the viewport from right to left.
Tilt Upwards Moves vertically upwards.
Tilt Downwards Moves vertically downwards.
Slow Spin Applies subtle rotational movement.
Diagonal Pan Moves diagonally while slowly zooming.
Beat Shake Adds dynamic shake effects around music beats.
Audio Pulse Scales the camera in sync with music amplitude.
Glitch Pulse Adds rapid digital jitter effects.
Static (No Motion) Keeps the media completely still.

Slide & Blend Transitions

Define how the video transitions from one clip to the next:

Crossfade (Blend) Smoothly blends the outgoing and incoming clips.
Slide Left The next clip slides in from the right.
Slide Right The next clip slides in from the left.
Slide Up The next clip slides upward from the bottom.
Slide Down The next clip slides downward from the top.
Slide Diagonal The next clip enters diagonally.
Fade to Black Fades out through black before the next clip.
Zoom Blend Uses a rapid zoom transition between clips.
Spin Blend Uses rotational movement during the transition.
White Flash Creates a bright flash during the transition.
Glitch Cut Adds a digital glitch-style transition.
Cut (None) Instantly switches to the next clip.
